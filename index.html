Petya Library

A private, self-hosted personal research library for TikTok references.

Petya Library is designed to let the account owner authorize access to available TikTok data through official TikTok APIs and organize available liked and favorited video references into a searchable personal library.

Available metadata may be categorized with locally hosted AI models. The application does not publish content, send messages, or modify the TikTok account.
